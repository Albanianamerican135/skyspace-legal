Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 6, 2026

SkySpace is a place to share what you're up to — photos, videos, and where you are — with people who follow you. These guidelines apply to everything you post: text, photos, videos, profile info, and any shared locations.

What's not allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content. This includes sexually explicit images or video, regardless of context.
  • Harassment or bullying. Don't target, threaten, demean, or repeatedly contact someone who doesn't want to hear from you.
  • Hate speech. Attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or serious disease have no place here.
  • Violence or graphic content. Don't post content that depicts, glorifies, or threatens violence, or that's intended to shock or disturb.
  • Spam. Repetitive, unsolicited, or automated posting — including fake engagement, scams, and misleading links — isn't allowed.
  • Impersonation. Don't pretend to be someone else, or misrepresent your identity or affiliation.
  • Illegal content or activity. Anything that violates local, national, or international law.
  • Sharing someone else's private information (their exact home address, phone number, etc.) without their consent.

How we handle reports

Anyone can report a post or profile directly from the app. Reports go to our moderators for review. Depending on what we find, we may remove the content, suspend the account, or take no action if the report doesn't hold up. You can also block anyone whose content you don't want to see, independent of reporting them.

Repeated or severe violations will get an account permanently suspended. We aim to be consistent, but we do use judgment — these guidelines describe the spirit of what's expected, not an exhaustive legal contract.

If you believe your account was suspended in error, contact jurimecospace@gmail.com.
