Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SkySpace collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: a stable, anonymous identifier from Sign in with Apple, plus the username, bio, and profile photo you choose to set. We never see your real Apple ID email unless you choose to share it and Apple provides it to us on first sign-in.
  • Content you post: text, photos, videos, and any location you choose to attach to a post.
  • Location: only requested when you tap "Add Location" on a post, or use the live map feature — and only while you're actively using that feature. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Usage relationships: who you follow, who you've blocked, and reports you've filed, so the app can function (e.g. showing your feed, hiding blocked accounts).

Where it's stored

All data is stored in Apple's CloudKit, in a container tied to this app. We don't run our own servers and don't share your data with third-party advertisers or analytics providers — there are none integrated into this app.

How it's used

Solely to operate the app: showing posts to your followers and the public feed, showing your profile to other users, enforcing blocks, and letting moderators review reported content. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Other users can see

Your username, bio, profile photo, and anything you post (unless they've blocked you or you've blocked them). Treat anything you post as visible to anyone using the app, unless you remove it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete individual posts, block other accounts, and permanently delete your entire account (and everything tied to it) at any time from Settings.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and account creation requires an Apple ID, which itself is subject to Apple's own age requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
